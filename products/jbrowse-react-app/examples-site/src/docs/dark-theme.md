The `configuration` prop takes a
[MUI theme palette](https://mui.com/material-ui/customization/theming/).
`palette.mode: 'dark'` switches the whole app to the built-in dark theme;
`primary` / `secondary` override individual colors.

DNA base colors and the rest of the options are in the
[theming guide](https://jbrowse.org/jb2/docs/config_guides/theme/).
