The rows are three records from the
[Cancer Genome in a Bottle](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.01.722316) HG008-T
structural variant benchmark, a pancreatic tumor line. `SV_75` removes both
copies of _CDKN2A_, one of the four genes recurrently altered in pancreatic
adenocarcinoma ([Waddell et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature14169)).
