Each track takes its `displayDefaults` height and `TrackStack` clips overflow,
so a track with many rows scrolls inside itself. Raise its `height` or add a
`ResizeHandle`, as in [track labels](../track-labels/). Put the stack in a
container that grows. More in
[Build your own](https://jbrowse.org/jb2/docs/embedded_components/#build-your-own).
