In grammar-of-graphics terms, Color by is a colour scale and Group rows by is a
facet, each over a field. `setColorScale` and `setFacet` take the same
`{ field, domain }`: the colour's `domain` assigns the palette in that order,
and the facet's stacks those bands first.
[Grouping and lane order](https://jbrowse.org/jb2/docs/config_guides/grouping_and_ordering/)
covers the facet on every display.
