# ADR-193: An adapter answers the mark pipeline its typed arrays

## Status

Accepted (2026-09-28). The last landing of the table pipeline,
behind [ADR-191](adr-191-the-mark-pipeline-runs-over-tables.md) and
[ADR-192](adr-192-a-span-answers-a-hover-by-its-row.md).

## Context

After ADR-191 the steps ran over tables, but every table started as one
`Feature` per row, and the encoder read every lane of a row in one walk
through a reader per channel. Two adapters already hold their rows typed: the
MAF worker packs each block's sequences into one byte arena for the MAF
display, and bbi answers a BigWig region as `starts`, `ends` and `scores`.
[ADR-152](adr-152-wiggle-stays-off-the-column-encoder-until-two-lanes-go.md)
kept wiggle off a column encoder over 20 retained bytes a feature, four of
them an identity `featureIndex`.

## Decision

- **`BaseFeatureDataAdapter.getFeatureTable(region, opts)`** is what
  `layerFeatures` and the row-matrix RPC fetch, `asTable(getFeaturesArray())`
  by default. `ColumnTable` (`@jbrowse/core/util/featureTable`) is the table an
  adapter answers from typed arrays: a column per field and an id per row.
- **Two column kinds carry what an adapter holds typed**: `text`, bytes in one
  buffer with an offset and length per row, and `list`, a row's entries as a
  range of an entries table, keyed by name where the list is a record. `flatten`
  over a list reads no entry, and `cells` walks bytes in place.
- **The MAF adapters** answer blocks over the packer's arena
  (`mafFeatureTable`, `plugins/maf/src/util/mafFeatureTable.ts`), with
  `alignments` and `empties` as keyed lists over the species rows. **The BigWig
  adapter** answers a `ColumnTable` over bbi's arrays
  (`bigWigFeatureTable`), whose ids and hover JSON are `BigWigFeature`'s. Its
  lanes are `subarray`s of bbi's buffer, and the encoder copies every lane it
  ships, so none of them reaches a transfer list.
- **The encoder fills a lane at a time.** One pass admits the rows over `x`,
  `x2` and `y` alone, straight off the lanes where all three are lanes with no
  index, and no pass runs where all three hold whole numbers, which are never
  NaN. Every other lane is then its own loop over the admitted rows: a number
  lane copied, a constant color or shape filled, a categorical over a
  `category` column resolved once per label and painted by code, and a reader
  only for what no lane holds. Each loop reads through one composed index, or
  none, and is written once for each, since a `kept ? kept[k] : k` in the body
  cost 3-4x in V8, which does not hoist the test.
- **`featureIndex` is absent where it is the identity**, when no row was
  skipped. `featureIndexAt(channels, i)` reads either. `facet.ts` gathers every
  lane of a layer with hidden sections through one list of kept instances.
- **`cells` hands its lanes on as views** of the buffers it walked into, not
  copies.

## Consequences

- **The encode is no longer the lever.** Timed stage by stage on ada over the
  470-species typed region, the encode takes 18 ms, the pack 32 and the steps
  167, of which the `cells` walk is 133. The span request runs at
  0.24x<!--m:typed-sources-maf-pipeline.maf-cells-typed-470-species.headVsBase-->
  of main's, and at
  1.26x<!--m:typed-sources-maf-display.470-species-200-blocks-of-250-columns.typedVsMaf-->
  the MAF display's own path.
- **The typed MAF table is level with the features it packs** at 470 species
  (1.08x<!--m:typed-sources-maf-display.470-species-200-blocks-of-250-columns.typedVsFeatures-->)
  and ahead over 20,000 narrow blocks
  (0.82x<!--m:typed-sources-maf-display.26-species-20000-blocks-of-8-columns.typedVsFeatures-->):
  the pack costs what the `MafFeature` reads saved. Parsing straight into the
  packer would spare the `MafFeature`, its record per species and the pack's
  second read, for the MAF display too.
- **ADR-152's first condition is met.** With `featureIndex` declined a bar's
  payload is `x`, `x2`, `y` and a color lane, 16 bytes a feature, which is
  its criterion's line of wiggle's 12 plus 4 rather than over it; and with no
  hit index the BigWig table encodes at
  1.05x<!--m:bigwig-table.raw-declined.tableVsWiggle--> wiggle, under its 1.10x
  line. The second condition, a constant color riding as a scalar, is still
  open, and the bar asks for a hit index wiggle's hover does without.
- **A bench that keeps a large heap alive measures its collector.**
  `tablePipeline.bench.ts` built its million synthetic features for every run,
  and each full collection the typed arm's buffers set off marked them: the
  typed MAF row read 471-506 ms against 371-403 ms for the features row until
  the synthetic inputs were built only for the scenarios that read them.
