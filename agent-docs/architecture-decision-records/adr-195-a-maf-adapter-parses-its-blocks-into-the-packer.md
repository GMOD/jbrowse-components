# ADR-195: A MAF adapter parses its blocks into the packer

## Status

Accepted (2026-09-28). The lever
[ADR-193](adr-193-an-adapter-answers-the-mark-pipeline-its-typed-arrays.md)
named in its consequences.

## Context

After ADR-193 both readers of a MAF region packed it into the one arena the
MAF display ships: the display's worker RPC, and `getFeatureTable` for the
mark pipeline. Both packed it out of `MafFeature`s, so every adapter first
built a record and a string per species, filed the records in a dictionary per
block, and handed the packer a feature to walk again with `for...in`. The
typed table was level with the `Feature` path at 470 species and ahead over
narrow blocks: the pack cost what the `MafFeature` reads saved.
[MAF_LARGE_BLOCKS.md](../reference/MAF_LARGE_BLOCKS.md) had measured
the worker's upstream half at 83% of its time on narrow blocks, where each
block is a few columns and every cost is per row.

## Decision

- **`MafAdapterBase.readBlocks(query, sink, opts)`** writes a region's blocks
  into a `MafBlockSink` (`plugins/maf/src/util/mafBlockSink.ts`): a block, its
  rows, its empties, each sequence as `text[from..to)`. By default it files
  each `MafFeature` of `getFeatures`, which is what `BgzipMafAdapter` and
  `BgzipTaffyAdapter` still do: the first turns a `-` reference block over by
  reverse-complementing its strings, and the second builds each row out of
  column lines.
- **`MafTabixAdapter` and `BigMafAdapter` override it.** `MafTabixBlockReader`
  scans the alignment column as `scanMafTabixEntry` does and hands the sink
  ranges of the column; `BigMafBlockReader` runs `applyMafLine`, whose rows
  now go to a `MafLineTarget`, and hands over each `s` line's sequence.
- **Both consumers are sinks.** `MafRegionSink` packs the visible rows,
  discovers every species and takes the reference row the reader names (a
  byte search named a haplotype identical to an unlisted reference), for the
  worker RPC; `MafTableSink` packs every row and keeps the block ids for
  `getFeatureTable`. `MafWirePacker` writes a range of a string.
- **`RecordSlots` keeps a record's order.** A species named twice in a block
  keeps its first place and its last entry, and an array-index name comes
  first, as the `alignments` record files them. `getFeatures` keeps its own
  parse, and `tabixBlockReader.test.ts`, `stanzaBlockReader.test.ts` and
  `speciesRows.test.ts` hold the two equal: the packed wire byte for byte, the
  reference row, the species order, and the table's ids and JSON.

## Consequences

- **Narrow blocks gain and wide ones do not.** From a MAF-tabix file the MAF
  display's worker takes
  0.80x<!--m:maf-parse-into-packer.26-species-20000-blocks-of-8-columns-maftabixadapter.directVsFeatures-->
  and the span
  0.78x<!--m:maf-parse-into-packer.26-species-20000-blocks-of-8-columns-maftabixadapter.typedDirectVsFeatures-->
  of their time over 20,000 blocks of 8 columns, and level over 1,600 blocks
  of 250 columns and at 470 species, where coverage and the `cells` walk are
  most of the request and the rows are 5-12 times fewer.
- **bigMaf gains less.** Its line grammar splits every line on whitespace and
  builds the `s` line's fields as strings, and the sink spares only what comes
  after: 0.90x<!--m:maf-parse-into-packer.26-species-20000-blocks-of-8-columns-bigmafadapter.directVsFeatures-->
  on narrow blocks. The split is the next cost on that path, and it is
  `BgzipMafAdapter`'s too.
- **One parser per format, since ADR-199.** MAF-tabix's and bigMaf's
  `getFeatures` is `readBlocks` into `MafFeatureSink`, and clustering by
  identity counts matches through a sink of its own; the old parse survives
  only as the tests' oracle, `legacyMafParse.fixture.ts`. Over narrow blocks
  from a MAF-tabix file `getFeatures` takes
  0.87x<!--m:maf-readers-over-readblocks.26-species-20000-blocks-of-8-columns-maftabixadapter.featuresRebuiltVsLegacy-->
  its time and the matrix
  0.61x<!--m:maf-readers-over-readblocks.26-species-20000-blocks-of-8-columns-maftabixadapter.identitySinkVsLegacy-->;
  bigMaf's `getFeatures` over wide blocks read 1.13x once and 0.90-1.07x on
  reruns, level within the noise the record's notes give. MAF-tabix's `alignments`
  records now list their fields in the order bigMaf's and the bgzip MAF's do.

- **The reader is now the largest item on narrow blocks.** Profiled on the
  laptop, `MafTabixBlockReader.read` is about a quarter of the MAF display's
  worker, most of it the six `indexOf` calls an entry takes. A `charCodeAt`
  scan of the entry's fields measured slower than those calls.
