# ADR-196: A bar answers a hover by its row

## Status

Accepted (2026-09-28). Follows [ADR-192](adr-192-a-span-answers-a-hover-by-its-row.md)
from the span to the value marks, and closes the hit-index gap
[ADR-193](adr-193-an-adapter-answers-the-mark-pipeline-its-typed-arrays.md)
left between a bar over a BigWig and wiggle.

## Context

After ADR-193 a bar over a BigWig encoded at 1.05x wiggle without the hit index
and 3.01x with it: the Flatbush (`hitIndexOf`) was most of the worker's time,
built on every fetch and again on the main thread whenever a facet hid a
section. Wiggle's own display hovers without any index. A bar, like a span,
stands between `x` and `x2` in one row, so the rows near the cursor and a
binary search over each answer what the Flatbush answered, except for the value
window, which the Flatbush also searched.

## Decision

- **`bar`, `rule` and `line` answer by rows** (`hit: 'rows'` in
  `markSpecs.ts`), so their layers ask for no `index` lane. `findMarkHit`
  builds `rowSpanIndex` over the layer the first time a hover asks, and keeps
  the instances of the rows near the cursor that meet the bp window and whose
  `y` the mark's value window holds, which is the Flatbush's search over
  `(x..x2, y)` without the rows out of reach. The rows reach out by the mark's
  size too, the most a rule or line's stroke stands past its band when rows are
  squashed below a pixel.
- **A point keeps the Flatbush.** A scatter crowds a bp window with points at
  every value, which the Flatbush prunes on value and a row's bp order cannot:
  a Manhattan plot at a whole-genome zoom stacks a column of points under
  every hover's bp window. **A link keeps it too**, since its curve spans rows.
- **Candidates go back to front** by instance, from either lookup, so of two
  equally near the one drawn over the other answers; the Flatbush's tree order
  decided that before.
- **The density tier** builds its Flatbush only for a mark that hovers through
  one (`hitsByIndex`), so a bar standing in for refused features builds none.
- **The parity check found three value windows narrower than the ink**, which
  both lookups shared: a bar below the domain paints its clip strip inside the
  band edge, out of the window; a bar standing on a baseline the radius
  reaches was cut to the cursor's side of it; and a rule's thickness was left
  out. `valueWindow` takes `edgePx` for the strip and opens both ways where the
  radius reaches the origin, and the rule widens its radius by half its size.
  `findMarkHit.test.ts` holds the row lookup, the Flatbush and every instance
  to one answer at random hovers over bars, rules and lines: rows in no order,
  a reversed block, a scroll, a row table that hides and reorders keys, a
  facet with a hidden section and the density tier.

## Consequences

- **A bar over a BigWig is level with wiggle** at the synthetic tier
  (1.00x<!--m:bar-row-lookup.2048-synthetic-row.tableVsWiggle-->) and within
  an eighth of it over raw rows
  (1.11x<!--m:bar-row-lookup.raw-row.tableVsWiggle-->), where it was
  3.26x<!--m:bar-row-lookup.raw-flatbush.tableVsWiggle-->. ADR-152's first
  condition, a bar's payload on wiggle's line, now holds for the time as well
  as the bytes.
- **The build moves to the first hover and shrinks**: 2.14ms<!--m:bar-row-lookup.raw-row.buildMs-->
  once on the main thread, against
  41.69ms<!--m:bar-row-lookup.raw-flatbush.buildMs--> in the worker on every
  fetch, and a hover asks it faster than it asked the Flatbush.
- **What the bp window misses stays missed**, by both lookups: a bar floored at
  `minWidthPx` past its bp, and a line's stroke caps, half its width past its
  bp: ink a pixel or half a stroke past the eight-pixel radius.
- A long bar early in a row keeps the walk back going over every bar after it,
  as for a span; a bar layer that overlaps itself heavily pays in hover time,
  never in fetch time.
